Do I need my PC or a server?

No. After install, the app is fully self-contained — it talks to Yahoo for quotes and to Tradovate directly for live futures data and orders.

Credentials are stored only on your device, never on any server. You can also use the read-only app ID flow most prop firms provide.

Any firm that issues Tradovate credentials works — Apex, Topstep, TPT, Tradeify and more. Enter them in setup and trade your funded account.
